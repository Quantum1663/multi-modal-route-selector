*Software Requirements Specification (SRS) for Multi-Modal Route Selector*

## 1. Introduction
### 1.1 Purpose
The Multi-Modal Route Selector aims to optimize cross-border shipping routes by integrating multiple transport modes (road, rail, sea, and air) with real-time data and AI-driven decision-making.

### 1.2 Scope
- Route optimization based on cost, time, and efficiency.
- Integration of real-time data (traffic, weather, customs clearance).
- AI-powered predictive analysis for potential delays and route adjustments.
- User-friendly interface for logistics planners and businesses.
- *Blockchain-Enabled Transparency* – Immutable records for logistics transactions.
- *Sustainability Optimization* – Carbon footprint tracking and eco-friendly route selection.
- *Integrated Supply Chain Financing* – Dynamic credit terms and payment solutions.

### 1.3 Definitions, Acronyms, and Abbreviations
- *AI* – Artificial Intelligence
- *ML* – Machine Learning
- *ETA* – Estimated Time of Arrival
- *API* – Application Programming Interface

### 1.4 References
- Industry research papers
- APIs for logistics and real-time data

## 2. Existing Solutions & Their Limitations
### 2.1 Current Market Solutions
- *Flexport* – Global freight forwarding platform.
- *FourKites* – Real-time supply chain visibility.
- *Project44* – Predictive analytics for shipment tracking.

### 2.2 Identified Limitations
- Lack of AI-driven adaptive routing.
- High costs for small-scale businesses.
- Limited cross-border compliance integration.
- Lack of real-time alternative route suggestions.

## 3. Proposed Solution & Unique Selling Proposition (USP)
### 3.1 Core Features
- AI-driven real-time route optimization.
- Multi-modal integration for seamless logistics.
- Cost-aware route planning with predictive risk assessment.
- User-friendly dashboard for better decision-making.
- *Blockchain-Enabled Transparency* – Secure and tamper-proof logistics records.
- *Dynamic Compliance Management* – Continuous updates on trade regulations.
- *Sustainability Optimization* – Eco-friendly routing and emissions tracking.
- *Predictive Analytics for Demand Forecasting* – AI-driven market trend analysis.
- *Integrated Supply Chain Financing Options* – In-platform financial support.

### 3.2 Unique Selling Proposition
- *AI-Powered Decision Making* – Intelligent route adjustments based on live data.
- *Predictive Risk Analysis* – Anticipating disruptions and suggesting proactive solutions.
- *Multi-Modal Efficiency* – Seamless integration of different transport modes.
- *Real-Time Cost Optimization* – Dynamic cost calculation based on market trends.
- *Blockchain Security* – Transparent and secure logistics transactions.
- *Eco-Friendly Logistics* – Carbon-conscious route recommendations.
- *Integrated Financing* – Support for cross-border financial transactions.

## 4. System Architecture
### 4.1 High-Level Design
- Data Collection Layer (Traffic, Weather, Customs APIs)
- AI Processing Layer (Predictive models for route selection)
- Blockchain Layer (Immutable transaction records)
- User Interface (Web-based dashboard for route selection)

## 5. Functional & Non-Functional Requirements
### 5.1 Functional Requirements
- User authentication and role management.
- API integration for real-time data.
- AI model for route prediction and cost optimization.
- Interactive dashboard for visualization.
- Blockchain integration for logistics transparency.

### 5.2 Non-Functional Requirements
- High-speed processing for real-time recommendations.
- Scalable cloud-based architecture.
- Secure data handling and encryption.

## 6. Technology Stack
- *Backend* – Node.js/Python
- *Database* – PostgreSQL (for geospatial data)
- *AI/ML* – TensorFlow/PyTorch
- *Frontend* – React.js
- *Blockchain* – Hyperledger or Ethereum
- *APIs* – Google Maps, Weather APIs, Customs Data APIs

## 7. Testing & Validation
- Simulated real-world logistics scenarios.
- Performance testing for response time and scalability.
- Accuracy testing for AI-driven predictions.
- Security testing for blockchain data integrity.

## 8. Deployment Plan
- Initial prototype for LogiTHON 2025.
- Cloud-based hosting for scalability.
- Continuous improvement based on real-world feedback.

## 9. Conclusion
The Multi-Modal Route Selector is a groundbreaking AI-powered logistics tool designed to revolutionize cross-border shipping through intelligent automation and optimization. With blockchain transparency, sustainability tracking, and financial integration, this solution sets a new industry standard.
